Where the PQC unit lives in Ara

A scalar instruction enters through CVA6, is expanded by Ara's dispatcher and sequencer, and is broadcast to the vector lanes. Inside each lane, the new vfu_pqc sits alongside the ALU and the FPU as a peer functional unit that reads from and writes back to the vector register file.

Inside vfu_pqc.sv

A gather → compute → scatter engine

Each VPQC instruction names an opcode that drives a table of word counts. The unit gathers that many 64-bit words from the VRF (one or two operands), feeds them to the selected primitive block, and scatters the result words back. All cryptographic computation happens here — the testbench only plays the scalar core and load/store unit.

How the 100 vectors are generated and checked

The official NIST AES-256 CTR-DRBG (SP 800-90A, no derivation function) drives the Known-Answer-Test process. An outer DRBG seeded with bytes 0…47 produces 100 per-test seeds; each seeds an inner DRBG that yields the keying material. A trusted pure-Python reference and the Ara hardware must agree byte-for-byte.
